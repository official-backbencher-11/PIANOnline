About the project

PIANOnline

A browser-based music playground built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and the Web Audio API.

What you can do

  • Play a two-octave-style virtual piano with mouse, touch or keyboard.
  • Control output volume and hide/show keyboard labels.
  • Record the generated piano audio directly in the browser without microphone access.
  • Replay and save piano performances.
  • Play and record an eight-pad drum kit.
